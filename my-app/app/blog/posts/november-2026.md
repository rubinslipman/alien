I recently found a flat that was in an area I loved. It's tiny, there's a few flats around it and it was a huge step for me. I am so thankful for Isi for encouraging this for me. The tragedy; I got a homebase survey done which is a level 2 survey with the mortgage lender and they contacted my mortgage advisor letting them know there is some structural issues with the flat and they wont lend. If i get a chartared survey done the results will indicate for them what the next steps are so under advise from the advisor I'll wait for the report, go back to the seller and ask them to fund the survey.

Had I not done this level 2 survey I never would have done known this was a an issue, it's both a blessing and a curse. My mum suspects the seller and estate agent may already be aware and were perhaps banking on me not doing the survey. Though I can't be certain of this. 

It's a hard end to a what was an exciting next step for me. I'll keep looking and hopefully find somewhere.
