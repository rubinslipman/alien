# Slow Mornings by the Coast

The first light reached the harbor before the town was fully awake. I walked past the old bakery and watched the water carry a line of silver across the bay.

There is a kind of rhythm to coastal mornings that asks you to slow down. Every cup of coffee tastes warmer when the wind carries the smell of the sea.

I followed the road past white houses and bright green hills, stopping where the cliffs dropped into softer water. Somewhere between the last house and the first stretch of open sand, I decided the day did not need to be planned.

By noon the sky had turned clear and the harbor was crowded with small boats. I sat near the water and wrote down the things I wanted to remember: the sound of gulls, the color of the tide, and the feeling of trying nothing at all.

The evening ended quietly, with orange light on the sea and a promise to return before the season changes.
