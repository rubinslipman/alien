# Mountains Before the Rain

The trail began in a field of pale grass where the mountains were still hidden behind soft cloud. I carried the map in my pocket and tried to remember every turn before the path became steep.

By midmorning, the forest changed from soft green to darker shade. The air grew cooler and the world felt carefully arranged around every slope and creek.

A line of small stones marked the place where the trail broke into a view of the valley. Below me, the last houses of the village looked almost untouched, and the mountains carried the kind of quiet that feels like permission.

The sky darkened slowly, first with a layer of gray, then with a deeper line of color along the ridge. I took shelter beneath a cedar tree and watched the rain gather over the pass.

The walk back felt different. The trail had become a memory of the day, full of water, light, shape, and distance.
