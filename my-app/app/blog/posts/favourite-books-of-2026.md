## Fourth Wing
20-year old Violet Sorrengail is the youngest daughter of General Lilith Sorrengail, who leads the Basgiath War College in the kingdom of Navarre. While Violet was trained to be a scribe and has weak joints, her mother orders her to risk her life and become a dragon rider. - wiki plot summary

Loved this book, though i read the whole series I have found the first book the best. I thought the writing was easy to follow, the sex was an interesting surprise. I enjoyed the world that Yarros has created and couldn't wait as I read for Violet to meet her dragon(s).

## Harry Potter series
Harry Potter was a film series that I watched growing up, I was boarding a plane back from Seville, Spain when I realised that i didn't have a film downloaded or anything to do so i jumped onto Spotify and quickly downloaded Philosopher's Stone. I was captivated from the moment it started. Fantastic, I got home and headed straight online to World of Books and bought the whole series.

## The Hobbit
I am currently reading this, driven to pick it up due to how impactful I found the Harry Potter series. Tolkien has a Yoda like way of writing which i find harder to follow, though the story and words paint a vivid picture of his world. 

## Dune
Dune, again I had watched the movie first and loved it so much I had to learn more about the world. I picked up Dune and was instantly pulled into the world the author created. However, I found that there was a lot of information, items, characters that I could not fully understand. I thought that it was perhaps a more difficult read than i would of liked. I prefered the way JK Rowling wrote.

## Red Rising
This book series is fantastic, i did stop at Morning Star though as i got distracted. 100% worth a read.

Harry Potter was my Favourite series of 2026.
